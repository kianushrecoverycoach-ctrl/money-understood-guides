Quick answers

What is in the free guide?

A plain-English comparison of three common types of life insurance: final expense, term, and whole life. What each one is built for, how premiums and coverage usually work, the trade-offs, and the questions to ask before you apply.

Which type is best?

It depends on what you want the coverage to do, how long you need it, and your budget. The guide is educational and does not pick a policy for you. A licensed agent in your state can talk through your situation.

Can anyone qualify?

Eligibility, underwriting, and pricing depend on the insurer, the product, your age, your health, and your state. Some policies ask health questions and some do not. Nothing on this page promises approval or a price.

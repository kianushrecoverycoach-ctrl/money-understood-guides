Quick answers

Is this from the TSP or the government?

No. Money Understood is an independent education channel. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the federal government, the Office of Personnel Management, the Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board, or the Thrift Savings Plan.

What is in the free guide?

A plain-English walkthrough of the main choices for a TSP balance when you leave federal service: keep it in the TSP, roll it to an IRA, roll it to another employer plan such as a 401(k), or take money out through single withdrawals, installments, or an annuity. It also covers tax considerations in general terms and a list of questions to ask before you decide.

Do I have to move my TSP?

No. Leaving your money in the TSP is one of the options, and the guide covers it alongside the others. Nobody should pressure you to move it.

You have 4 options

For a TSP or 401(k) left with a previous employer, the general choices are:

  1. Leave it where it is. Many plans let you keep your balance in the plan after you leave, subject to plan rules.
  2. Roll it into your new employer's plan. If your new plan accepts roll-ins, this can keep your savings in one place.
  3. Roll it into an IRA. An IRA may offer different investment choices, fees, and rules than an employer plan.
  4. Cash it out. Withdrawals are generally taxed as income, and if you are under 59½ you may owe a 10% early withdrawal penalty unless an exception applies.

Each option has trade-offs in fees, investment choices, taxes, and protections. This is general education, not a recommendation, and no outcome is guaranteed.
